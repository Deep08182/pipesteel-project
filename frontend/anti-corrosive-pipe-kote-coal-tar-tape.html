Product Description

Pipe Kote Coal Tar Tape as per IS is a hot applied coal tar tape for underground pipelines. It is manufactured as per AWWA-C-203 & IS 10221-82 & IS 15337 specification with special plasticized coal tar enamel, sandwiching tough fiberglass fabric with thermo HMHDPE film on both sides, which provides high anti-corrosive protection to underground oil, gas, chemical, effluent, and water supply pipeline systems.
