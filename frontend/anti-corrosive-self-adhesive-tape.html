Product Description

Pipe Kote Self Adhesive Anti Corrosive Anti Rot & Anti Bacterial Bitumen Tape is a cold-applied self-adhesive bituminised tape for underground pipelines, manufactured as per EN 22592 & AWWA-C-209. It features a sandwiched construction with tough fiberglass reinforcing fabric and thermo HMHDPE film on the upper side, backed with a peel-off release film on the underside. It provides high anti-corrosive, anti-rot, and anti-bacterial long-term protection for underground piping networks.
