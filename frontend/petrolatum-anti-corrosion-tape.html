Product Description

Petrolatum anticorrosion tape uses a non-woven fabric base impregnated with petrolatum-based compound made from petroleum, which has good elasticity, and closely sticks to any shape. It forms an excellent anti-rust / anti-corrosion barrier with simple hand winding, application, and smoothing. Ideal for protecting underwater and underground pipe flanges, valves, irregular welded tees, and structural tie-rods.
