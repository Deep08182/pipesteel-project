Product Overview & Description

"Ar Aluminium top self adhesive membrane is widely used & excellent product for waterproofing of rooftops & joints & shades."

Ar Aluminium Top Self Adhesive Membrane 1.5 mm is a high-performance, multipurpose, all-weather self-adhesive bituminous waterproofing membrane. Protected with an aluminium film top finish, it offers exceptional UV resistance, thermal protection, and instant leak-proofing for rooftops, expansion joints, structural joints, and industrial shades.

Key Features

1 Self-adhesive Instant peel-and-stick application without torching.
2 Easy to apply Requires no special heating tools or primers for smooth surfaces.
3 Waterproof 100% impermeable barrier against rainwater & hydrostatic head.
4 UV resistant Aluminium top film shields bitumen compound from UV degradation.
5 All-weather performance Resists heavy rain, extreme heat, and severe frost conditions.
6 Aluminium-film top High-reflectivity silver surface reduces heat absorption.
7 Polyester reinforced High tensile matrix prevents tearing under structural movement.
8 Pressure-sensitive Bonds stronger as pressure is applied to substrate.
9 Permanent sealing Long-lasting watertight bond for decades of service.
10 Temp (-20°C to 100°C) Does not crack in extreme cold or melt under hot sun.

Usage & Applications

Roof Waterproofing Sealing sheet overlaps, metal roof joints, asbestos & corrugated roof sheets.
Solar Panel Sealing Weatherproofing around solar mounting legs, cable penetrations & frame joints.
Wall Sealing Sealing parapet wall junctions, window frame surrounds & exterior masonry gaps.
Terrace Waterproofing Sealing roof drain outlets, terrace floor joints & expansion joints.
Basement Waterproofing Sealing underground wall joints, retaining walls & foundation pipe sleeves.
Ducting & HVAC Air-tight sealing of metal ventilation ducts & industrial exhaust piping.
Construction Joints Sealing precast concrete panel gaps, gutters & valley flashing.
Crack Sealing Emergency repair of active roof cracks, chimney collars & rainwater downpipes.
General Waterproofing All-purpose maintenance and waterproofing for industrial & domestic sites.
