Product Description

Coal Tar Enamel as per IS:10221 & AWWA-C-203 (CTE) is a thermoplastic polymeric coating produced from the plasticization of coal tar pitch, coal, and its distillates followed by the addition of inert mineral fillers. It offers exceptional adhesion to metal substrates, chemical resistance, and cathodic disbondment resistance for underground buried pipes and storage tanks.
