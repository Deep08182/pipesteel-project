Product Description

Anti Root Water Proofing Membrane with spun bonded polyester mat at its core materials. It has an excellent resistance to weather & ageing. It also has very good tensile strength, elongation & superior lap joint strength. App (Atactic Polypropylene) is an excellent resistance to hot & cold temperature along with special resistance to penetration of Roots through the membrane, Roots gets diverted without puncturing the membrane when Bituseal Anti root is in use. Bituseal Anti Root is a very good choice for Terrace Garden & other areas where root growth is expected.
