Product Description

A R Bituminous Aluminum paint is a multi purpose coating anti corrosive & water proofing paint with aluminum finish, it is made of Oxidized bitumen, special formulated aluminum compound with volatile solvents. Bituminous Aluminum paint is used to reflect UV rays and protect bituminous waterproofing membranes, felts & fabrics. It is also used as protective coating on iron, steel, masonry & other surfaces.
