ABOUT US

Leaders in Pipe Protection & Waterproofing Since 1991

A.R. Laminators, established in 1991, is one of the leading service providers in the pipe protection industry. Our pipe protection services enhance the strength of underground pipelines by coating and wrapping them with anti-corrosive material — including corrosion coatings, waterproof coating, and rust-proof primer.

Our services are used to coat and wrap national water lines, oil lines, gas lines, and sewage lines across India. Led by Mr. Amit Desai, the company adopted high-end pipeline protection technology and works closely with clients to deliver customized taping & wrapping solutions — including on-site application at the vendor’s/client’s location.

Our sister concern, A. R. Industries (est. 2007), extends our offering to waterproofing membranes, bituminous coatings, heat shrink sleeves, emulsion bitumen, and bridge & deck waterproofing services.
